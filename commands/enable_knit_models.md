---
description: Enable specific models for Loom agents
---

Use the `enable_knit_models` tool to enable specific models for Loom agents.

**Usage:** `/enable_knit_models <provider/model> <provider/model> ...`

Always use the exact `provider/model` identifier shown in the `list_knit_models` output.

**Example:** `/enable_knit_models openai/gpt-4.1 anthropic/claude-3-5-sonnet`
