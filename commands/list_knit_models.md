---
description: List available models and their enabled/disabled status
---

Use the `list_knit_models` tool to list all discovered models with their exact `provider/model` identifiers, cost, context window, reasoning capability, and current enabled/disabled status, plus the proposed tier assignment plan.

No arguments required.

To change which models Loom agents can use, use `enable_knit_models`, `disable_knit_models`, or `reset_knit_models`.
