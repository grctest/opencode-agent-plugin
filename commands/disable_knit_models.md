---
description: Disable specific models for Loom agents
---

Use the `disable_knit_models` tool to disable specific models so Loom agents will not use them.

**Usage:** `/disable_knit_models <provider/model> <provider/model> ...`

Always use the exact `provider/model` identifier shown in the `list_knit_models` output.

**Example:** `/disable_knit_models openai/o1`
